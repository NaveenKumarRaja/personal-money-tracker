Privacy Policy

Money Tracker (Personal Assistant) · Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what the Money Tracker app ("the app") collects, why, and how you can delete it. The app is published by NaveenKumar Raja ("we"). Contact: naveenk090199+moneytracker@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and the name you enter at registration. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never stored by the app in readable form.
  • Financial records you enter: income and expense amounts, dates, descriptions, categories and subcategories.
  • App settings: currency, date format and theme.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, bank or card details, or device identifiers for advertising. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: signing you in, showing your records, and backing them up and syncing them between your own devices. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Your records are stored on your phone and in a cloud database run by our service provider, Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services, Singapore region). Supabase processes the data only on our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules in the database ensure each account can read and change only its own records. Your sign-in token is kept in encrypted storage on your phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from the live database right away. Copies may remain in our provider's backups for a limited period until those backups expire.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your records, from the cloud and from that phone.

Without the app: email naveenk090199+moneytracker@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: naveenk090199+moneytracker@gmail.com.
