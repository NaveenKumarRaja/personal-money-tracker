Personal Money Tracker

Track income and expenses offline. Syncs securely across your devices.

⬇ Download for Android

Free · Android 7.0 or newer · about 60 MB · all versions

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  3. Tap Install, then open Personal Assistant and create an account.

This app is not on Google Play yet, so Android shows a warning for apps installed from a website. To update later, download the new version here and install it over the old one; your data is kept.

What it does

  • Record income and expenses in seconds, even with no internet
  • Dashboard: this month's income, expenses and balance
  • Spending by category and subcategory, with charts
  • Search and filter your full history
  • Your data is backed up and synced to your other devices
  • No ads, no tracking. Delete your account any time from Settings
